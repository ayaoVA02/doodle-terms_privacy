Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Doodle-app, operated by Yao VA (the "Service"), handles information. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

1. Information we collect

The Service does not require visitors to create an account and does not collect personal information from viewers of our videos. When the account owner connects TikTok or YouTube, we receive only what the platform's authorization provides: basic account identifiers (such as your account ID and display name) and the access token needed to upload videos on your behalf.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to upload and manage videos on the connected account. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely on the operator's own system and are never published. We keep them only as long as needed to run the Service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Information is shared with TikTok or Google only as needed to publish videos through their official APIs.

5. Your choices

You can revoke the Service's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) or your Google account's third-party access page. You can also email us to delete any information we hold about you.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
